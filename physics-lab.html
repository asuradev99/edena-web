SIX EXPERIMENTS · ONE LIBRARY

Physics you can drag.

Orbits integrated through the CPU particle seam, O(N²) gravity solved on the GPU, two chaotic systems, a two-source interference field, and the vibrational modes of a drumhead. The n-body panel runs its own compute and render pipelines over ping-pong storage buffers; the rest are ordinary WebGPUView panels sharing one device. Drag the 3D panels to orbit, scroll to zoom.

01

Kepler orbits, integrated

createParticleState → ParticleSimulation.step → reset

CPU SEAM

Three planets with eccentricity 0, 0.35 and 0.65, all with GM = 1, advance through the library's ParticleSimulation — a shared state, an acceleration callback, and a reset() that restores the initial buffers exactly. The rings are coloured by distance; raise Δt far enough and the integrated path visibly drifts off its ring.

02

Gravity, all-pairs, on the GPU

GpuParticleSimulation({ mode: 'nbody', layout: 'disc' })

N-BODY
measuring GPU step…

One thread per body loops over every other body — no tree, no neighbour grid, no atomics: raw O(N²) gravity with Plummer softening. Positions never leave the GPU; the render pass instances them straight out of the storage buffer, and the timing above is measured with timestamp queries. The disc begins on circular orbits, so the seeded noise is free to grow into spiral arms.

03

Two pendulums, 0.1° apart

ramp() → per-vertex colors → merge()

CHAOS

Both pendulums obey identical equations and start one tenth of a degree apart. The traces are coloured by time through a custom ramp(), so the instant the two histories diverge shows up as colours that stop matching.

04

The Lorenz attractor

RK4 → gradientTube(polyline bands)

CHAOS

ẋ = σ(y − x), ẏ = x(ρ − z) − y, ż = xy − βz with σ = 10, β = 8/3. Below ρ ≈ 24.7 the trajectory spirals into a fixed point; above it the two lobes take over. Drag the slider and the curve is re-integrated and redrawn.

05

Two-source interference

colorMappedSurface(amplitude, …, plasma, [48, 48])

WAVES

Two coherent sources add with 1/(1 + r) falloff, and the result is drawn as a height field with its value carried in colour. The hyperbolic nodal lines along the midline are where the two contributions cancel.

06

Modes of a drumhead

functionSurface(f, x, y, [48, 48]) + Group.visible

WAVES

The clamped square membrane z = sin(mπx/2)·sin(nπy/2) on [−1, 1]², built with functionSurface and coloured by displacement. Higher modes add nodal lines; the checkbox toggles a Group's visible flag, and the surface breathes through a Timeline driven by tick() instead of an absolute seek.

Coverage: the CPU particle seam (createParticleState, ParticleSimulation, reset, acceleration callbacks) and the GPU particle core (mode: 'nbody', ping-pong storage buffers, instanced-from-storage rendering, timestamp-query timing, reset); RK4 and semi-implicit integration; geometry primitives and merged tubes; per-vertex colours from ramp and colormaps; polyline, sphere, circle, merge, axes3d; orthographic and perspective cameras; projected labels; and Timeline (cues, tween-driven reveals, and a tick()-looped vibration); functionSurface alongside parametricSurface and colorMappedSurface; Group.visible; and the view option alphaMode. Add ?samples=1 to drop MSAA, ?dpr=1 to cap resolution, or ?alpha=opaque to skip premultiplied compositing on a slow backend.
