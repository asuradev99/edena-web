k path & sampling

k is fractional in reciprocal vectors b₁,b₂, with aᵢ·bⱼ = 2πδᵢⱼ. Custom cells may need a custom symmetry path.

One-atom hexagonal (triangular) lattice: six equal nearest neighbors, one band, ε₀ = 0. Hopping uses Hᵢⱼ = t, so negative t places Γ at the band minimum.

Dispersion and Fermi contours cover the first Brillouin zone. Drag the 3D dispersion to orbit. Other views stay flat; drag to pan. Scroll to zoom. The filled Fermi slice shows E(k) ≤ μ for the displayed band; its gold boundary is E(k) = μ. Occupation is spinless, in states per cell. DOS is a sampled histogram.
